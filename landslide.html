Landslide Safety

Know warning signs and avoid slopes during heavy rain.

Landslides can happen after prolonged rain or ground changes. If officials advise evacuation, leave promptly.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Watch for signs

  • New cracks in ground or walls
  • Leaning trees or posts
  • Unusual water flow or rumbling sounds

Stay safer

  • Avoid steep slopes during intense rain
  • Know alternate routes
  • Report warning signs to local authorities
  • Follow evacuation instructions
