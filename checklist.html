Emergency Checklist

Use this checklist to review basic supplies.

This checklist is printable. Check each item as you gather or verify it.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Go-bag basics

  • Water
  • Ready-to-eat food
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • First-aid supplies
  • Personal medicines
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Copies of important documents in waterproof pouch
  • Whistle, mask, hygiene supplies
  • Cash and emergency contact card

Household reminders

  • Keep supplies accessible
  • Check expiration dates
  • Consider babies, older adults, pets, and disability-related needs
