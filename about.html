About SAN DIG

Preparedness information made easier to understand.

SAN DIG is a student website concept that organizes basic disaster-preparedness reminders into simple, accessible pages.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Our goal

Help users take practical steps before disasters, learn safe actions, and find official updates.

Design approach

  • Plain language
  • Readable contrast and large controls
  • Responsive layout for phones and computers
  • Images include descriptive alternative text
