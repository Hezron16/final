Typhoon Safety

Prepare for strong winds, heavy rain, and possible flooding.

Monitor official weather updates, secure loose outdoor items if safe, and prepare for power interruptions.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Before

  • Follow PAGASA updates
  • Charge phones and power banks
  • Prepare water, food, medicine, and flashlights
  • Know evacuation routes

During

  • Stay indoors away from windows
  • Avoid going outside during strong winds
  • Do not cross floodwater

After

  • Wait for official all-clear
  • Avoid downed wires and damaged structures
  • Check on family members safely
