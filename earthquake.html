Earthquake Safety

Know what to do during shaking and after it stops.

Practice safe actions and identify sturdy cover and safe exit routes in your home or school.

Open checklistExplore hazards

During shaking

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On
  • Protect your head and neck
  • Stay away from windows

After shaking

  • Check for injuries and hazards
  • Expect aftershocks
  • Leave damaged buildings carefully when safe
  • Follow official updates
