Evacuation Guide

Leave early when authorities advise you to evacuate.

Know where to go, how to get there, and what to bring. Never delay evacuation to collect belongings.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Before leaving

  • Follow official evacuation notices
  • Bring your go-bag and essential documents
  • Tell family members where you are going

On the way

  • Use official routes
  • Avoid floodwater and damaged roads
  • Assist others only if safe to do so

At the center

  • Follow staff instructions
  • Keep household members together
  • Check in with local authorities
