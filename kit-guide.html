Go-Bag Guide

Pack supplies your household may need during evacuation.

Use a sturdy bag and customize it for household needs, weather, and any official guidance.

Open checklistExplore hazards

Suggested supplies

  • Water and food
  • Flashlight and batteries
  • Radio
  • First-aid kit and personal medicines
  • Whistle
  • Masks and hygiene items
  • Copies of important documents
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Extra clothing and sturdy footwear

Keep it ready

Store the bag in an easy-to-reach place and review it regularly.
